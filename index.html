About Us

Reliable Lighter Supply, Built for Global Business

FLYSEA GLOBAL INC. is a U.S.-based trading company specializing in the wholesale supply of lighters. Through our established supplier network in China, we provide a wide selection of open flame, single jet, dual jet, triple jet, quad jet, torch and utility lighters for distributors, wholesalers and retailers.


We work closely with reliable manufacturers to maintain stable supply, consistent product quality and competitive wholesale pricing. OEM and private-label options are available for selected models, including custom colors, patterns, logos and packaging.
